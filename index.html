CH 09How I think

Awareness is easy. Adoption is the job.

Three rules behind every post and every reply.

01

Use it first

People can tell when you haven't. I don't write about anything I haven't touched.

02

Write for the newcomer

If someone new can't follow the post and take the next step, it didn't do its job.

03

Stay after the post

The replies and the Discord are where trust gets built. I'm there.
